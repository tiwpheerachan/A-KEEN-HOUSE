## Notes for the build

- **TBD prices**: The menustic source had limited price coverage for non-signature items. Either fetch fresh pricing from a current LineMan/Grab listing for the One Bangkok branch, or display the menu without prices and link to delivery apps for current prices. Both are acceptable for the demo — the goal is to look credible, not perfectly current.
- **Signature flag**: Both signature items (Burnt Caramel Latte, Classic Brekkie) deserve visual prominence — bigger image, callout treatment.
- **Categories on the page**: Order should be Coffee → Sandwiches → Desserts, not menustic's order. Coffee leads because that's the brand's primary identity.
- **Drink imagery is gold**: A KEEN HOUSE's Instagram has gorgeous shots of every signature drink. Use these as the menu hero images, not stock photos.
