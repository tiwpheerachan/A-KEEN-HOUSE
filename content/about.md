## Brand essence

A KEEN HOUSE is a small, premium specialty coffee chain in Bangkok with four locations across the city's best malls and a standalone original shop in Sukhumvit 40. The brand pairs a refined coffee program — single-origin drips, signature creations like the Burnt Caramel Latte and the Macadamia White Chocolate Hojicha Latte — with a thoughtful menu of open-faced sandwiches and patisserie-style desserts. The aesthetic is minimalist with warm wood, dark accents, and dramatic lighting. The voice is confident but unpretentious. The audience is brand-aware urban Bangkok.

## Suggested /about page narrative

**(Approx. 250 words — tighten as needed)**

A KEEN HOUSE began as a single café on Soi Sukjai, tucked into Sukhumvit 40. What started with a few hand-picked beans and an open kitchen has grown into four homes across Bangkok — One Bangkok, Paragon, Emsphere, and the original — but the philosophy hasn't shifted: thoughtful coffee, thoughtful food, thoughtful spaces.

The menu pairs a serious coffee program with a quietly inventive food side. The Burnt Caramel Latte became the signature drink almost by accident; the Macadamia White Chocolate Hojicha Latte and the Coconut Cream Matcha keep the curious coming back. On the food side, the open sandwich format gives full attention to the ingredient — the Sauté Mushroom with Garlic and Herbs, the Roasted Pumpkin with Spiced Cream Cheese, the Classic Brekkie. Desserts lean refined: Lemon Crème Brûlée, Red Wine Poached Pear.

Each location carries the same DNA — dark wood, warm lighting, vertical lines, a counter you can sit at and watch coffee being made — but adapts to its setting. The mall locations move faster; the Sukhumvit 40 original is where you slow down.

If you've found us on Instagram, you've already seen most of the work. The site is here for the practical things: where we are, when we're open, what's on the menu this season, and how to order in if you can't make it out today.

*— A KEEN HOUSE*

## Voice & tone guidelines

| Trait              | Show this                                                | Avoid this                          |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Confidence         | "Where the Burnt Caramel Latte was born."                | "We hope you'll enjoy our coffee."  |
| Warmth             | "A place to slow down."                                  | "Premium customer experience."      |
| Craftsmanship      | "Thoughtful coffee, thoughtful food, thoughtful spaces." | "Award-winning, world-class."       |
| Specificity        | Name the drink, name the dish, name the soi.             | Generic "delicious food" filler.    |
| Restraint          | Short sentences. White space. One idea at a time.        | Long-winded descriptions.           |
| Bilingual nuance   | Mix Thai phrases sparingly where natural.                | Forcing Thai for the sake of it.    |

## Phrases that already exist in their world (use them)

- "Brewed to Perfection"
- "#AKEENSIGNATURE"
- "Summer—poured, plated, and served" (current campaign — date-stamp accordingly)
- "Specialty coffee" / "Open sandwich" / "Signature drinks"

## Phrases to NOT invent

- Do not fabricate origin stories about the founder. We don't have those facts. Keep the about copy outcome-focused.
- Do not claim awards or rankings ("Bangkok's #1 coffee" etc.). Verify before claiming.
- Do not name baristas, roasters, suppliers — verify before naming people.
