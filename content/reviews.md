# Customer Reviews — Sentiment & Sample Quotes

> Sourced from Google reviews, Wongnai, Lemon8, Trip.com, and Wanderlog. Use these to: (1) extract recurring themes for marketing copy, (2) pick 2–3 short quotes for a "What people say" testimonial section, (3) understand what customers actually love so the site emphasizes those things.

---

## Recurring praise themes (for hero / about copy)

The most consistent things customers love:

1. **Atmosphere & decor** — "warm and inviting", "minimalist home-style design", "cozy charm", "beautiful decoration", "lighting on the columns"
2. **Coffee craftsmanship** — "great coffee", "many options", "great hospitality", "barista pours drip coffee carefully"
3. **Drink creativity** — pistachio latte gets specific praise; coconut cream variants are Instagrammable
4. **Sandwich quality** — "delicious food", "unexpectedly delicious"
5. **Service** — "great hospitality", "Extraordinary with great product knowledge"
6. **Vibe consistency** — works for solo work, dates, group hangs

## Sample short quotes (paraphrased and trimmed for fair use)

These are summaries / paraphrased renderings, NOT direct quotes — verify and request permission before using verbatim quotes from real customers in production.

> "Great coffee, many options, cozy and comfortable atmosphere — and great hospitality."
> *— Customer review, One Bangkok branch*

> *— Customer review*

## Negative / mixed signals (for risk awareness)

Surface very rarely. Most common minor critiques:

- Mall locations can be busy at peak times (expected for the format).
- Pricing is on the premium end — this is consistent with the brand and not a complaint.
- Sukhumvit 40 has earlier closing hours (5:30 PM) which surprises some visitors expecting a typical café.

## Use in design

- **Testimonial section:** 3 short paraphrased quotes max. Don't put more than 3 — feels desperate.
- **Hero overlay:** Could use one short quote layered over an interior shot (e.g., *"warm, inviting, comfortable"*).
- **Trust signal:** "4.9 ★ on Google · 544 reviews" prominently displayed for the One Bangkok flagship.

## ⚠️ Copyright & ethics note

Direct verbatim reproduction of customer reviews can be a copyright issue and is bad form. The Trojan Horse demo site should:
- Use **paraphrased / summarized** versions of sentiment, OR
- Use **the customer's full handle and a link back** to the original review if quoting verbatim, OR
- Skip testimonials entirely and rely on the Google rating badge + IG embed (which IS the social proof).

For the demo, I recommend the third option — Google rating + IG embed handles social proof without IP risk.
