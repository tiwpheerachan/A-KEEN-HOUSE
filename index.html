Our House

A coffee house, four homes.

A KEEN HOUSE began as a single café on Soi Sukjai, tucked into Sukhumvit 40.

What started with a few hand-picked beans and an open kitchen has grown into four homes across Bangkok — One Bangkok, Paragon, Emsphere, and the original — but the philosophy hasn't shifted: thoughtful coffee, thoughtful food, thoughtful spaces.

The Burnt Caramel Latte became the signature drink almost by accident. The Macadamia White Chocolate Hojicha Latte and the Coconut Cream Matcha keep the curious coming back. On the food side, the open sandwich format gives full attention to the ingredient — sautéed mushroom, roasted pumpkin, the Classic Brekkie. Desserts lean refined: Lemon Crème Brûlée, Red Wine Poached Pear.

Each location carries the same DNA — dark wood, warm lighting, vertical lines, a counter you can sit at and watch coffee being made — but adapts to its setting. The mall locations move faster; the Sukhumvit 40 original is where you slow down.
